TASK:

Build Mission Control as the top-level operational oversight dashboard for the Plexus enterprise platform.

Current desired screen:
Mission Control should show:
- top-level operational oversight across platform domains
- active facility selector
- Super Admin access preview
- global patient search
- RingCentral live/API status
- execution spine summary
- operational lanes
- lane workbench
- today’s ancillary operations
- calls and communication
- clinic operations
- patient services
- clinic team metrics
- finance and revenue
- operations and logistics
- clinic success metrics
- RVU tracking
- role queue board
- Plexus Chat overlay

Important:
Do not make Mission Control a fake static dashboard. It must be wired to real platform data where available, with honest empty states where data is not available.

Architecture rules:
- Mission Control is the admin/executive command center.
- It is not Team Member Portal.
- It is not Engagement Center.
- It is not Playground.
- It is not Patient Directory.
- It should oversee the full execution spine across clinics, teams, patients, calls, documents, reports, billing, and revenue.
- patient_execution_cases is the source of truth for assigned operational work.
- patient_journey_events is the timeline/audit history source.
- Team Member Portal remains where ACS/PCS work call lists.
- Engagement Center remains where work is assigned.
- Mission Control shows oversight and blockers.

Core data spine:
Mission Control should aggregate, where available, from:
- patient_execution_cases
- patient_journey_events
- patients / patient directory source
- clinics/facilities
- team members / roster
- call outcomes
- scheduling status
- document/report status
- billing readiness
- revenue/profit assumptions
- role queues
