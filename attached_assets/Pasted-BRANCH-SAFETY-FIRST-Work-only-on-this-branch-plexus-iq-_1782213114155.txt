TASK:

Redesign Engagement Center UI to feel modern, premium, intuitive, and iOS-like.

Current problem:
The Engagement Center looks like an old Windows 1999 dashboard. It has bucket-heavy UI at the top and too many raw dates stacked down the page. It is not attractive or intuitive. Remove the ugly bucket-heavy top section and redesign the whole experience around assignment, outreach readiness, and patient action.

Core product purpose:
Engagement Center is the assignment and outreach command center.
It is where admin/manager reviews qualified patient cases, understands why the patient needs outreach, assigns the work to ACS/PCS/team members, sets priority and next action, and sends the case into Team Member Portal call lists.

Architecture rules:
- Engagement Center assigns work.
- Team Member Portal is where ACS/PCS works call lists.
- patient_execution_cases is the source of truth for assigned call work.
- Engagement Center must write assignment state to patient_execution_cases.
- Do not use /api/scheduler-assignments for Engagement Center-assigned call work.
- Do not resurrect old Outreach Center.
- Do not route users to scheduler cards or old outreach pages.
- Do not change schema unless absolutely required.
- Do not break existing assignment logic.

PART 1 — Remove / replace bucket-heavy top UI

Find the current bucket/status section at the top of Engagement Center.

Replace it with a clean premium command header and compact summary strip.

Do not show giant bucket cards across the top.

New header should include:
- Engagement Center title
- subtitle: Patient outreach assignment and engagement control
- clinic/facility filter
- team member/team filter if available
- status filter
- search patient input
- compact summary metrics:
  Ready to Assign
  Due Today
  Follow-up
  Callbacks
  Blocked

Use a clean iOS-like style:
- white cards
- soft gray background
- rounded corners
- strong typography
- subtle borders/shadows
- minimal badges
- no clutter

PART 2 — New main layout

Redesign the page into a clean 3-zone command center:

Left:
Smart filter rail

Center:
Patient engagement worklist

Right:
Selected patient/case preview and assignment panel

Layout:
- Left rail should be compact but readable.
- Center worklist should be the primary focus.
- Right panel should show selected case details and assignment actions.
- Avoid dense tables unless used inside a clean card.
- The page should feel premium and easy to scan.

PART 4 — Patient worklist

The center worklist should show patient/case rows or cards.

Each patient row/card should include:
- patient name
- call reason
- target test/procedure/ancillary
- clinic/facility
- priority
- due/next action in human terms
  examples:
  Overdue
  Today 2:00 PM
  Tomorrow
  This week
- assigned team member or Unassigned
- last call outcome if available
- cooldown/outreach eligibility if available
- Clinician PDF status if available
- Plexus PDF status if available

Actions on each row/card:
- assign
- set priority
- set next action
- open patient
- open PDFs/proof
- send/update in Team Member Portal call list if existing logic supports it

Do not make the list just dates going down the page.
Group dates intelligently:
- Overdue
- Today
- Tomorrow
- This Week
- Later
