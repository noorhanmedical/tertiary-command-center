RVU & PLEX FACTOR COMPENSATION

Create a full module called:

RVU & Plex Factor Compensation

This manages operational productivity, team payouts, payout rules, and Plex Factor bonus logic.

Submodules:

1. RVU Dashboard
2. Service RVU Settings
3. Plex Factor Rules
4. Role Payout Rates
5. Clinic / Region Overrides
6. Service Event Ledger
7. Compensation Calculator
8. Payout Review
9. Payroll Integration
10. RVU Audit Log

Default RVU values:

- BrainWave: 9 RVUs
- VitalWave: 6 RVUs
- Ultrasound: 4 RVUs
- EKG: 3 RVUs
- Urinalysis: 2 RVUs
- PGx: 5 RVUs

Default payout rates:

Technician:
- base payout: $1.00 per RVU
- Plex Factor payout: $2.00 per RVU

Remote team:
- base payout: $0.10 per RVU
- Plex Factor payout: $0.20 per RVU

Plex Factor default logic:

Plex Factor activates when:

- same patient
- same service date or encounter
- 2 or more eligible ancillary services completed

Default Plex Factor multiplier:

- 2x

Eligible services by default:

- BrainWave
- VitalWave
- Ultrasound
- EKG
- Urinalysis
- PGx

Important:
Plex Factor is not just a label.
It must behave like real calculation logic in the prototype.

Plex Factor calculation steps:

1. Identify patient
2. Identify service date / encounter date
3. Count completed eligible services
4. If count is greater than or equal to threshold, activate Plex Factor
5. Apply multiplier to eligible services only
6. Calculate technician payout
7. Calculate remote team payout
8. Store calculation with audit detail

Example:

Patient receives BrainWave + VitalWave same day.

BrainWave = 9 RVUs
VitalWave = 6 RVUs
Total = 15 RVUs

Without Plex Factor:
Technician payout = 15 × $1 = $15
Remote payout = 15 × $0.10 = $1.50

With Plex Factor:
Technician payout = 15 × $2 = $30
Remote payout = 15 × $0.20 = $3.00
